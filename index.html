Full name:
Civilian Assistance & Rescue Emergency Services

Type:
Emergency assistance concept

Purpose:
Connecting people in need with appropriate assistance.

Overview

C.A.R.E.S. stands for Civilian Assistance & Rescue Emergency Services.

C.A.R.E.S. is an emergency assistance concept designed to connect civilians who need help with the appropriate rescue or response network.

The system focuses on communicating two important things: where help is needed and what type of help is required.

The Problem

During emergencies, people may struggle to communicate what kind of assistance they need and where they need it.

At the same time, rescue teams may have to deal with many requests coming from different locations.

C.A.R.E.S. aims to make this connection more organized by bringing emergency requests and response information together in one system.

How C.A.R.E.S. Works

  1. A civilian selects "I NEED HELP."
  2. They choose the type of assistance they need.
  3. Their location and emergency type are sent to the connected rescue network.
  4. Rescue teams can see requests on a live rescue map.
  5. Responders can use the information to help coordinate the appropriate assistance.

Types of Assistance

  • Medical — medical assistance
  • Water — access to drinking water
  • Food — emergency food assistance
  • Shelter — temporary shelter assistance
  • Evacuation — assistance leaving an unsafe area
  • Missing Family Member — reporting a missing family member
  • Trapped — reporting that someone is trapped

The C.A.R.E.S. Rescue Map

The rescue map is an important part of the C.A.R.E.S. concept.

Emergency requests can be displayed on a map using different markers for different types of assistance. This can give responders a visual overview of where help is being requested.

The map is currently a prototype concept for this student project.

Our Mission

"WHERE HELP IS REQUIRED, C.A.R.E.S. CONNECTS."

Our mission is to create a better connection between people who need assistance and the response networks that can help them.

Our Goal

Our goal is simple:

Connecting people in need with the right assistance, at the right time.

Future of C.A.R.E.S.

C.A.R.E.S. could potentially expand to support more communities, more rescue networks, and a wider range of emergency situations.

The concept could also become more advanced as the technology and emergency-response network develop.
